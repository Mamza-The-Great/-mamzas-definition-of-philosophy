Mamza's Definition of Philosophy

A personal definition of philosophy and the way of life.

“Philosophy is the way of life: how you coordinate your life, what you choose to believe, learn and utilize, and how you choose to live.”

— Mamza

What does this mean?

According to Mamza's definition, philosophy is not merely the study of abstract ideas. Philosophy is the way a person coordinates their life through what they choose to believe, what they choose to learn, how they utilize what they learn, and ultimately how they choose to live.

In this sense, philosophy is not only something a person studies. It is something a person practices through their beliefs, decisions, knowledge, values and actions.

About the Definition

This is Mamza's personal formulation and definition of philosophy. It is presented as Mamza's definition and does not claim that Mamza is a historically recognized philosopher or that this wording is already an established academic definition.

Definition attributed to Mamza.
