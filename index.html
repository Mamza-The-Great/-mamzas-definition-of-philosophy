Mamza's Definition of Philosophy

A personal definition of philosophy and the way of life.

“Philosophy is the way of life: how you coordinate your life, what you choose to believe, learn and utilize, and how you choose to live.”

— Mamza

What does this mean?

According to Mamza's definition, philosophy is not merely the study of abstract ideas. Philosophy is the way a person coordinates their life through what they choose to believe, what they choose to learn, how they utilize what they learn, and ultimately how they choose to live.

In this sense, philosophy is not only something a person studies. It is something a person practices through their beliefs, decisions, knowledge, values and actions.

About the Definition

This is Mamza's personal formulation and definition of philosophy. It is presented as Mamza's definition and does not claim that Mamza is a historically recognized philosopher or that this wording is already an established academic definition.

My Dream: A Global State

I have a dream of a united humanity: a peaceful Global State in which people and nations can put aside the divisions that keep us apart and work together for the good of everyone.

My vision is not one of conquest or bloodshed. I believe humanity's greatest achievements can come through peace, cooperation, knowledge, justice and shared purpose.

I dream of a world that works seriously toward reducing war, poverty, suffering, hunger and unnecessary division. A world where our differences do not prevent us from uniting when unity can make human life better.

I believe humanity has the potential to accomplish extraordinary things when we choose cooperation over hatred and shared progress over division.

This dream is connected to my philosophy because I believe that what we choose to believe should influence what we choose to learn, how we use what we learn, and ultimately how we choose to live.
